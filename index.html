Vision for the Game

I want this game to be about building something that you feel that you earned in a fun and challenging way. In the beginning of the game when you open the screen there will be levels and different buildings to work on and develop. Press on the first building/level and the more points you win on the pong part of the game it will go towards your progress on teh game. I hope for this game to bring motivation to the users to keep going hard to gain more points in the gamne to advance to new levels and create beautiful buildings and scenary.
